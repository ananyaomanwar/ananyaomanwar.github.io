Ananya Omanwar

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at Queen's University, working with Fady Alajaji and Tamás Linder. My research lies in information theory and probability, with a particular interest in generalized information measures and channels with memory.

My doctoral work examines how channel symmetry can simplify Sibson capacity calculations, and how information measures can bound the loss in estimation performance when observations are degraded.

Previously, I completed my MSc in Mathematics at IISER Mohali under the supervision of Neeraja Sahasrabudhe, studying opinion dynamics and the effects of external influence. I obtained my bachelor's degree in Mathematics from St. Stephen's College, University of Delhi.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy baking, knitting and photographing what I make.
